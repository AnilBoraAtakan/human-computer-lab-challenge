A TWO-MINUTE INTRODUCTION

Meet Luma.

  1. Make eye contact. Start the session and face the camera. I’ll wake up after a short moment. Look away for two seconds and I’ll settle.
  2. Give me a memory. Hold up a mug or another everyday object. Click the mic, say “Remember this,” then click again.
  3. Put it out of sight. Ask “What color was the object I showed you?” I’ll use my earlier observation.
  4. Give me a goal. Show it again and say “Look toward the mug, then check whether it’s still there.” I’ll move, take a fresh look, and report what I see.
  5. Celebrate. Ask for a little music. Motion, light, and melody come together.

The head-direction estimate is a coarse attention signal, not precise eye tracking. My simulated light cannot illuminate your physical room.
